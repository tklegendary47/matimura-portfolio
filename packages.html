02 — Questions Owners Ask

Before you reach out, here's what usually comes up.

How fast can I actually get a site live?
Tier 01 sites are delivered in 24–48 hours once I have your business details and photos. Tier 02 and 03 run 3–5 days depending on scope.
Do I own my domain and my site?
Yes. Domains are registered under your name or with clear written ownership terms — never locked permanently under my account with no handover path.
What if my business needs something bigger than these tiers?
Custom builds — bookings systems, e-commerce, member portals — are scoped and quoted individually, drawing on the same full-stack engineering behind the platforms on my Work page.
How do I pay?
Via EcoCash — 50% deposit to begin, the remaining 50% on delivery, before the site and domain go live.
