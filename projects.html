AEO Citation System

Search is changing shape. Buyers now ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, and Google AI where to go, not just Google — and most businesses have no idea whether they're being mentioned at all. The AEO Citation System is a diagnostic SaaS platform that tracks and explains a business's presence across those five engines, turning an invisible problem into a measurable, fixable one.

As Technical Lead, I own the product architecture: a Node.js / Express backend, MongoDB data layer, and the marketing site that introduces the platform to its first customers. Daniella Isiuwa leads operations from Nigeria, and together we built a 24-week partnership plan spanning research, technical foundation, and a phased path to launch — currently entering Phase 1: Learning & Research.

TalentTrack

TalentTrack is a fully responsive job board platform built entirely in vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no framework scaffolding to lean on. Listings are dynamic and JSON-driven, filtering happens in real time as a candidate narrows their search, and bookmarked roles persist locally through localStorage.

Accessibility was treated as a requirement, not a nice-to-have: semantic structure, keyboard navigation, and color contrast were built in from the first commit. TalentTrack was conceived not just as a course project but as a white-label product — a job board any local business could deploy as its own.
